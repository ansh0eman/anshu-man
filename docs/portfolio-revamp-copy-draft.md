## About

**Eyebrow:** the person behind the projects

**Heading:** about me, more or less.

I studied computer science at Manipal and now work with AI and data systems. I like building things, but I also like finding out what happens after the nice demo: who can use the thing, what breaks when the data is wrong, and whether the idea still makes sense outside my laptop.

That curiosity has taken me from image and music experiments to compliance workflows, internal tools, and an Apple Watch app with a suspiciously familiar green dial. I don't think those interests are as unrelated as they first look. I like figuring out how a complicated thing works, then making it understandable enough to use or play with.
